About

Analyst in the office. Captain in the pool.

I build financial models for commercial real estate, design immersive learning tools, and lead teams in and out of the water.

At Ransom Everglades in Coconut Grove, my academic interests center on business and real estate. That has taken me from analyzing property investments and housing markets to building generative-AI workflows for virtual reality at the University of Miami.

Outside the classroom I captain water polo, founded the Leadership Alliance, serve on the student board of the Coconut Grove Food Pantry, and spend as much time as I can underwater with a camera.
